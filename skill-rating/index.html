How your Skill Rating is calculated

We use every match in which you were in the playing XI over the last 3 seasons of the qualifying leagues (SPL, CBL, NPL, YPL) and KNPL, from your verified CricHeroes profiles. The latest season counts most (1.00, then 0.85, then 0.70). Each of the five parts below is scored out of 100.

55%PerformanceYour MVP points per match, batting, bowling and fielding together.
15%Win ImpactMatches your team won where you were one of its top 2 MVP contributors.
10%AwardsPlayer of the Match and tournament awards from the CricHeroes Heroes tab.
10%ConsistencyShare of your matches with 1 or more MVP points.
10%ExperienceMatches played in the playing XI; 30 matches gives full marks.

The weighted total of the five parts, rounded off to the nearest whole number, is your Skill Rating out of 100 (0.5 and above rounds up). Your category, Draft Price and Draft Point all use this rounded Skill Rating. KNPL-1 to KNPL-3 are not used.

Categories, base price and base point

Every player is placed by his own Skill Rating against the same fixed bands. There is no limit on the number of players in any category. Conqueror and Dominator need at least 8 MVP matches (matches in which your name appears in the match MVP list); below that, a player is placed in Rising Star Elite.

Draft Point = Base Point + Base Point × (Skill Rating ÷ 100)Draft Point is then rounded up to the next multiple of: 100 (1,000–2,000) · 200 (2,000–8,000) · 400 (8,000–10,000) · 500 (above 10,000).Example: a Dominator Apex player with Skill Rating 71 → Draft Price ₹6,000 + ₹6,000 × 0.71 = ₹10,260 · Draft Point 8,000 + 8,000 × 0.71 = 13,680 → rounded up to 14,000 Pts
